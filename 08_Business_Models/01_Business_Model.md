# 💰 Business Model & Economics

> [!INFO] Revenue Logic
> Commission-based + Value Added Services (VAS). Loop economics drive margin expansion.

## 💵 Revenue Streams
1. **Platform Commission:** 8% - 12% of Freight Value (Tiered).
2. **VAS:** GPS Tracking, Insurance, Loading/Unloading Labor.
3. **Finance:** Float interest on settlements (T+1 vs T+7).
4. **Penalty:** Late fees on loading/unloading (>4 hrs).

## 📉 Cost Structure
- **Variable:** Payment Gateway Fees, SMS/Notifications, Server Costs.
- **Fixed:** Engineering, Ops, Admin.
- **COGS:** Driver Payouts (Pass-through).

## 🔄 Loop Economics
- **Standard Trip:** Margin = Commission - Ops Cost.
- **Loop Trip:** Margin = (Commission Out + Commission In) - (Ops Cost - Savings).
- **Savings Source:** Reduced Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) for return leg.

## 🧾 Settlement Logic
- **Customer:** Pay Advance (50%) + Balance (on POD).
- **Driver:** Pay Advance (40%) + Balance (on Settlement).
- **Platform:** Deduct Commission before Driver Payout.
- **See:** [[03_ReturnTrip_Algorithm]] for loop discounts.
