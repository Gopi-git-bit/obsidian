# 📢 Marketing Applications

> [!GOAL] Acquisition & Retention
> Leverage operational excellence (SLA, Loops) as marketing hooks.

## 🎯 Target Segments
1. **SME Manufacturers:** High frequency, price-sensitive.
   - *Hook:* "Save 20% with Return Trip Discounts."
2. **Enterprise Supply Chain:** Need compliance & audit.
   - *Hook:* "Immutable Audit Logs & GST Compliance."
3. **Driver Partners:** Need reliability & timely payout.
   - *Hook:* "Guaranteed Return Loads & Fast Settlement."

## 🛠 Marketing Tech Stack
- **CRM:** HubSpot / Salesforce (Integrated via n8n).
- **Communication:** Twilio (SMS/WhatsApp), SendGrid (Email).
- **Analytics:** Mixpanel / PostHog (User behavior).
- **Automation:** n8n (Lead nurturing workflows).

## 📣 Campaign Strategies
### 1. The "Empty Leg" Campaign
- **Message:** "Don't drive back empty. Get guaranteed return loads."
- **Channel:** Driver App Push + SMS.
- **Metric:** Driver Activation Rate.

### 2. The "SLA Guarantee" Campaign
- **Message:** "Late delivery? Auto-dispute & credit."
- **Channel:** B2B Email + LinkedIn.
- **Metric:** Enterprise Lead Conversion.

### 3. Referral Program
- **Mechanism:** Loop Group ID sharing.
- **Incentive:** Commission discount for referring new shippers.
