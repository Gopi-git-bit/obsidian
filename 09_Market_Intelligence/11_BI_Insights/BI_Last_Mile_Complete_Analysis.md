### Stage Details
| Stage | Function |
|-------|----------|
| **Macro Hub** | Regional inventory aggregation, bulk sorting |
| **Data Engine** | AI routing, predictive demand, partner data sharing |
| **Micro-Hub** | Urban localized distribution centers (high density) |
| **EV Execution** | Zero-emission final transit to customer |
